# Owner guide — using the desk every day

You don't need any technical knowledge for this part.

## The 30-second tour
* **Dashboard (My Command Center)** — start here. The dark card at the top is the single next thing that needs you. Tap **Complete**, **Reschedule**, **Call** or **WhatsApp** right on it.
* **+ button (phone) / New task (computer)** — add a task any time. Press **N** on a keyboard.
* **Search** — ⌘K / Ctrl+K (or the search bar) finds clients, tasks, notes, documents and linked emails.
* **Bottom bar (phone):** Home · Tasks · **+** · Calendar · More (Clients, Interviews, Gmail, Documents, Notifications, Settings).

## Add a task by just saying it
1. Tap **+ → Describe it**. Type — or tap the microphone — something like:
   *"Call Karen tomorrow at 3 PM to prepare him for his U.S. visa interview. Remind me five hours before."*
2. Tap **Preview task**. Claude fills in the title, category, client, date, time and reminders.
3. **Read it.** Anything yellow says **Please check** — a client matched by first name, a time that wasn't stated, a date like "next Monday" that can mean two things, a reminder that's already in the past, a similar task that already exists.
4. Fix anything, then press **Confirm & save task**. Only now is it saved, added to the calendar, and its reminders scheduled.

If AI isn't set up, use the **Manual** tab — it does the same thing by hand.

## Reminders
* Choose any mix: at the time, 15 / 30 minutes, 1 / 5 hours, 1 / 2 days, 1 week, or your own amount. Defaults live in **Settings → Reminders**.
* Reminders are sent by the **server**, so they arrive even when the app is closed — as long as alerts are turned on for that device (Notifications → **Turn on alerts**) and the scheduler is running (Settings → Setup status).
* Tap **Snooze** on the notification (Android/desktop), or use the clock button on any task.
* **Notifications → Delivery log** shows every attempt — sent, failed, expired — with the reason.
* If you move a task to another day, its old reminders are cancelled and new ones are created automatically.

## Interviews
**Interviews → Schedule interview**: choose the client, date/time, embassy and reminders (defaults: 2 days, 1 day, 5 hours, 1 hour before). It appears on the calendar, in the interview list and on the dashboard with a live countdown. Tick the preparation checklist, set the preparation status, record the outcome afterwards (which closes the task and moves the case forward).

## Clients, notes, documents
* **Clients → New client.** A document checklist is created for you. Change the pipeline status in *Case details*; the progress bar at the top updates.
* **Notes tab:** log a call, WhatsApp or meeting; pin important notes; tap the wand to turn a note into a task ("Client requested a call on Thursday" → follow-up reminder).
* **Documents tab:** drop files in, or tap **Upload** next to a missing checklist item. When all required items are in, the interview's *Documents* badge turns green. Files are private — only reachable while you're signed in.

## Gmail
**Gmail → Connect with Google** once. You'll see unread client emails, can search by client, and **link** a conversation to a client (it is never linked automatically — the app only *suggests* a match). Save an attachment into a client's documents, or make a task from an email. The app can read mail but can never send or change it.
*AI summaries* are off until you turn them on in Settings → AI & privacy.

## Keyboard shortcuts
`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` or `/` search · `N` new task · `G` then `D` Dashboard, `T` Tasks, `C` Calendar, `I` Interviews, `L` Clients, `M` Gmail, `O` Documents, `N` Notifications, `S` Settings.

## Privacy controls (Settings → AI & privacy)
Switch AI task capture, sharing client names with Claude, email summaries and day suggestions on or off individually. Passport images and document files are never sent to AI.

## Your data
* **Settings → Download JSON** exports everything.
* **Client page → Archive** hides a client and stops their reminders; **Erase permanently** deletes them and all their files (you must type their name).
* **Settings → Auto-delete documents of closed cases** — optional, off by default.

## If something looks wrong
* **No reminder arrived:** Notifications → Delivery log. "No active device" → turn on alerts on that device. "Subscription expired" → turn alerts off and on again. Settings → Setup status → *Reminder scheduler* must say "Last ran … minutes ago".
* **Gmail says "reconnect":** Google ended the connection (see the 7-day note in the README). Press Reconnect.
* **An AI preview looks wrong:** just edit the fields — or use Manual. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
